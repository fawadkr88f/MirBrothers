Our Sourcing Philosophy

Why We Shortlist These Plots

We know that selecting the right plot is just as important as selecting the right builder. The wrong soil type, complex legal encumbrances, or non-possession delay parameters can increase your construction gray structure budget by millions or freeze your capital entirely.

That's why we regularly review the market and identify selected possession-ready plots that may be suitable for clients who are planning to build. We look at soil stability, municipal zoning, possession status, plot characteristics (corner, park-facing, heights), asking price, and overall suitability before including a property in our daily selection.

Shortlisting Vetting Rules:

  • ✓ Possession Vetted: Files or unballoted plots are auto-excluded.
  • ✓ Immediate Construction: Sourced only from buildable blocks.
  • ✓ Competitive Valuation: Compared against phase averages.
  • ✓ Phase Diversity: Spread across DHA Phase 1 to Phase 11.
